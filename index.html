The reference summit for Spanish talent. Quality over quantity.

What the summit opens up for you, depending on where you sit.

  • Direct access to Bay Area investors already backing companies founded by Spaniards.
  • Introductions that are matched on purpose, never left to a badge in a crowd.
  • A stage to present your company to the partners and press who move things.
  • Time with founders a few steps ahead on the same road, from Spain to the Valley.
  • A first look at the Spanish founders building for the AI era.
  • Two days with the deal flow that usually costs a dozen flights to Madrid and Barcelona.
  • Room to invest alongside the Bay Area funds already active in Spain.
  • A direct line to the people shaping Spain's investment climate.
  • A seat among the executives and policymakers setting Spain's technology agenda.
  • Frank, off the record conversation with Silicon Valley counterparts inside Google HQ.
  • A place to align public and private strategy on AI and talent.
  • A lasting relationship with the Bay Area, not a single visit.

Two days, by invitation, built around three ideas.

A small, deliberate gathering for the people writing Spain's next chapter. Not a conference floor.

WHEN
Spring 2027
WHERE
Google HQ, Mountain View, California
FORMAT
Two days, by invitation

Inspire

We carry these role models back to Spain's universities, so the next generation grows up knowing what is possible.
